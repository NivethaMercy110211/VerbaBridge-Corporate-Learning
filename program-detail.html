Program Overview

The Executive Business English & Global Communication curriculum is engineered specifically for multinational corporations, technology leaders, and consulting enterprises where clarity and executive presence directly impact operational efficiency and client trust.

Unlike generic language apps or traditional ESL grammar courses, our methodology immerses corporate participants into simulated workplace scenarios: running high-stakes cross-border syncs, delivering persuasive financial & technical proposals, handling tough Q&A sessions from transatlantic stakeholders, and writing crisp, diplomatic executive summaries.

Who This Is For
Department heads, project managers, technical leads, consultants, and high-potential managers working across global regions.
Enterprise Impact
Reduces communication bottlenecks by 42%, cuts rework from ambiguous briefing, and accelerates cross-region project delivery.

Syllabus & Module Breakdown

6 Modules • 12 Live Masterclasses • 6 Applied Labs

  • Eliminating filler words, corporate jargon overload, and passive rambling.
  • Structuring sentences using the Executive Pyramid Principle (Lead with the conclusion).
  • Nuanced modal verbs for diplomacy vs. decisive leadership directives.
  • Applied Simulation: 3-minute executive briefing to global leadership board.

  • Meeting framing: setting clear objectives, alignment checks, and timeboxing.
  • Intervention techniques: diplomatically interrupting, refocusing digressing participants.
  • Managing multi-accent audio and fast-paced North American & European discourse.
  • Applied Simulation: Multi-stakeholder budget reallocation debate with real-time video feedback.

  • Translating complex engineering data into clear executive value propositions.
  • Storytelling arcs for product demos, client pitches, and steering committees.
  • Handling hostile audience inquiries and critical objections with poise.
  • Applied Simulation: Delivering a high-stakes 7-minute slide presentation to an external client committee.

  • Writing emails that get read and acted upon immediately (The 5-Sentence Rule).
  • Tone calibration: balancing firmness and warmth when delivering bad news or deadline warnings.
  • Slack/Teams etiquette for global asynchronous collaboration across timezones.
  • Applied Simulation: Drafting a crisis escalation update to C-suite and enterprise clients.

  • Tactical empathy: framing concessions and building win-win agreements in English.
  • De-escalating heated vendor conflicts and cross-department misalignment.
  • Closing statements, conditional phrasing ("If you can... then we are willing to...").
  • Applied Simulation: Live contract renegotiation roleplay against a senior trainer.

  • 360-degree post-training oral and written proficiency evaluation.
  • Individualized CEFR progression benchmark score and feedback dossier.
  • VerbaBridge Corporate Accredited Certification issuance to HR dashboard.
